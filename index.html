Official Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy for Nav Pulse

Last Updated: November 2026

Nav Pulse ("we", "our", or "us") is designed as a local-first, privacy-respecting financial utility for tracking mutual fund portfolios, SIP performance, and daily NAV metrics. We strongly believe your financial data belongs exclusively to you.

🔒 100% On-Device Portfolio Processing

Nav Pulse processes all mutual fund statements, folio transactions, and portfolio valuations strictly inside your device's local sandbox. We do not operate remote user databases or upload your financial records to cloud servers.

1. Data Storage & Local Persistence

  • Local SQLite Database: All portfolio holdings, transactions, and scheme metrics are stored locally on your device using a private SQLite database.
  • No Account Registration: Nav Pulse requires no account creation, email sign-up, or phone number authentication. You can use the app completely anonymously.
  • Cloud Auto-Backup Disabled: The application explicitly configures android:allowBackup="false" to prevent device backups from inadvertently mirroring your personal financial records to external cloud backups.

2. Mutual Fund Statement Import (CAS)

To compute accurate portfolio metrics and capital gains, Nav Pulse allows you to import official Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) in PDF, CSV, or Excel formats from recognized depositories (CAMS, KFintech, Zerodha):

  • In-Memory Processing: Statement parsing, regex extraction, and transaction reconciliation are executed 100% locally on your device CPU.
  • Zero External Transmission: Your PAN number, folio numbers, transaction amounts, and investor names extracted from imported statements never leave your device.

3. Network Connections & Permissions

Nav Pulse requires the following Android permissions strictly for the operational requirements of the MVP:

A. Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET)

  • Purpose: Querying public, official market feeds (such as daily AMFI NAV data, Yahoo Finance index values, and MFAPI endpoints).
  • Outbound Scope: Outbound requests contain only public scheme identifiers (e.g., AMFI scheme codes or market ticker symbols). No personal information, folio details, or portfolio quantities are included in network requests.

B. Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: Delivering optional, user-configured market alerts and daily NAV refresh updates.
  • Control: You may grant or revoke notification permissions at any time via Android System Settings.

4. Application Telemetry & Analytics

To monitor application stability, detect crashes, and improve responsiveness, Nav Pulse bundles Google Firebase Analytics (operated by Google LLC).

Strict Financial Data Sanitization:
Our telemetry pipeline contains automated safeguards that strictly filter out and strip any sensitive keys or values (including but not limited to amount, balance, pan, folio, units, price, nav, gain, and cost).

Firebase Analytics collects only pseudonymous, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry:

  • Anonymous app interaction events (e.g., screen navigation, button taps).
  • Device diagnostics (e.g., OS version, device model, crash stack traces).

Telemetry Consent: Users maintain complete control over analytics collection. Telemetry can be enabled or disabled at any time from the app settings menu.

5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Request

Nav Pulse is designed to give you complete sovereignty over your financial data. We provide clear, straightforward mechanisms for requesting and executing the deletion of any data associated with your use of the application:

A. Local Financial & Portfolio Data

All mutual fund transaction logs, folio details, scheme quantities, and valuation histories are stored solely in a private SQLite database inside the app's local sandbox on your physical device. We do not maintain any cloud databases or remote user accounts.

  • Immediate Self-Deletion: You can permanently wipe 100% of your financial records instantly by navigating to Settings > Apps > Nav Pulse > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data on your Android device.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the Nav Pulse application automatically triggers the Android OS to permanently erase the local database and all associated application files.

B. Telemetry & Analytics Deletion Request

Nav Pulse does not collect any personal financial numbers or user account credentials. To monitor crashes and app performance, we use Google Firebase Analytics which records only pseudonymous technical signals (such as app navigation events, crash logs, and random app instance identifiers).

If you wish to request the deletion of any pseudonymous telemetry data associated with your installation, you may submit a request to our support desk:

  • Request Email: khsupport1@gmail.com
  • Email Subject: Data Deletion Request - Nav Pulse
  • Information to Include: Your request to delete telemetry data along with your Firebase App Instance ID (accessible via app settings or by providing your device model and approximate installation date).
  • Processing Timeframe: Deletion requests are processed within 30 days via Google Analytics / Firebase User Deletion protocols.
  • Immediate Local Reset: You can also immediately disassociate your device from all past telemetry by clearing app data or disabling analytics under application settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Aside from public market data sources (AMFI) and Google Firebase Analytics, Nav Pulse does not incorporate advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or data brokers.

7. Children's Privacy

Nav Pulse is a financial utility intended for adult investors and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Since all financial calculations occur locally, parents can be confident that no personal information is collected from minors.
